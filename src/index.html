ABOUT THE FIGURES

How to read this planner

Applications and allocations are counted at the end of Stage 2. A family may appear at multiple settings. The figures describe historical demand. They cannot predict an individual offer or show live vacancies.

The directory follows Belfast council boundaries in the 2026/27 workbook. Earlier returns are joined by school number. Programme names and separate full-time or part-time records follow each year’s source. Missing age-group records are shown as unavailable.

Only Botanic and McArthur have manually reviewed criteria summaries here, both for 2026/27. Check the new criteria for every setting before completing a 2027 application.
